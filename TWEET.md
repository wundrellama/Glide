# Glide announcement

OMARCHY GLIDE — community fork maintained by wundrellama. Originally built by Fred Nix.

One mouse. One keyboard. Every Omarchy machine on your desk.

Move across a screen edge and your pointer and keyboard follow. See nearby Omarchy desktops, verify them over SSH, and Glide installs itself on the verified machine before applying your layout.

Arrange up to nine machines in a visual 5×3 desk map. Connections stay on the physical LAN and use authenticated DTLS. Clipboard contents stay local unless you opt in.

Optional text-on-crossing: disabled by default, enabled per adjacent pair from the panel with one toggle. Cross an edge and the machine you left pushes its clipboard text (only, up to 1 MiB) to the machine you enter — no files, no images, no continuous sync. Requires non-interactive public-key SSH in both directions and wl-clipboard.

If the pointer is on the wrong machine, move the sending machine’s physical mouse or press its keyboard. Glide immediately releases the remote handoff and centers the pointer back home.

Pause from the Omarchy bar. Emergency return: Left Ctrl + Left Shift + Esc.

One desk. Every Omarchy machine.
